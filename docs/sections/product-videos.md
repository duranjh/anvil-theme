# Product videos — `sections/anvil-product-videos.liquid`

_Section card, 2026-10-15. Wave 2. Built from `docs/agent-prompts/product-videos.md` (CONVENTIONS, spec card, chassis API, exemplars)._

## Purpose
A row of up to 12 video cards, each with an optional product under it: title, price and an add button. It suits short demo clips, unboxings and customer clips on the product landing page (SPEC §5 puts it between testimonials and the timeline) or the home page. Spec: `docs/SPEC.md` §4.2 "Product videos" (up to 12 cards, video or URL plus a linked product, layout, 9:16 by default, muted autoplay on phones). Design row: `docs/DESIGN-sections.md` §3 `anvil-product-videos` (video cards + add to cart, local `video` ×12, grid or slider, slider + cart JS, facade, controls visible, no autoplay with sound).

Phones show 1 or 2 cards per row, tablets at most 3, and desktop 2 to 6. The slider is a scroll-snap row that works without JavaScript. The grid wraps cards onto new rows.

## Settings
The 13 chassis settings come first, pasted verbatim from CONVENTIONS §4. Then come 14 section settings under the headers "Product videos", "Product under each video" and "Slider". That makes 27 settings in total (the limit is 40).

`schema.name` is "Product videos" (14 characters), `max_blocks: 12`, `disabled_on.groups: ["*"]`. Slider autoplay (auto-advance) is fixed at `0`: a row that moves on its own while videos play would be too much motion, so there is no setting for it.

## Markup and behaviour
- **Structure:** `anvil-section-head`, a visually hidden `<h2>` (`anvil.product_videos.heading`) when the heading is blank, then `<anvil-product-videos class="av-product-videos">` holding either `<anvil-slider class="av-product-videos__slider">` or `<ul class="av-product-videos__grid">`. Each card is `.av-product-videos__card`: a `<figure>` with the `anvil-media` box (`.av-product-videos__media`) and the `<figcaption>` (visually hidden when titles are off), then `.av-product-videos__product` (thumbnail, `<h3>` product title link, price, buy area).
- **Heading levels:** product titles are `<h3>` under the section `<h2>`, or under the hidden one (CONVENTIONS §3).
- **Card shape:** `anvil-media` offers no 9:16 box, so the section sets `aspect-ratio: var(--av-pv-ratio)` on its media boxes (two-class selector, so it wins over `.av-media`). `--av-pv-ratio` is written inline from the `ratio` setting. For 3:4, 4:5 and 1:1 the matching `anvil-media` ratio is passed too, so the box is right even without that rule.
- **Columns:** inline vars `--av-pv-cols-m|t|d` (Liquid), switched at 750 and 990 px. The grid reads them through `--av-pv-cols`. In the slider, the section sets the component's `--n` from the same vars at 750 and 990 px, so tablets show at most 3 tall cards rather than the phone count (`anvil-slider` has only a phone and a desktop value). `sections/anvil-testimonials.liquid` sets `--n` at tablet width the same way. The slider measures its page size from the layout, so arrows and dots follow. **Peek:** when the cards fit in view (`blocks ≤ columns` at that width), Liquid adds `av-product-videos--fits-m` (all widths), `--fits-t` (≥ 750 px) or `--fits-d` (≥ 990 px), and the CSS sets `--av-slider-peek: 0px` on the track, the slides' nearest ancestor, which wins over the inline value on `<anvil-slider>`. The default preset (4 cards, 4 per view) therefore fills the desktop row instead of leaving a 32 px strip on the right.
- **Video sources:** `anvil-media` decides. An uploaded video renders with `video_tag` (`playsinline`, `preload="metadata"`, controls on, poster at the last `widths` value: 750 px, or 900 px with one card per row on phones). Cards below the first row defer it (see Loading). A YouTube/Vimeo link renders the click-to-load facade (`youtube-nocookie` / Vimeo `dnt=1`; nothing loads before the tap; the iframe then plays with sound because the shopper asked for it). With only a cover image, the card shows that image. With nothing set, it shows the `lifestyle-1` / `lifestyle-2` placeholders in turn.
- **Uploaded videos only.** Everything `assets/anvil-product-videos.js` does applies to uploaded videos. After the tap, a YouTube or Vimeo facade becomes a cross-origin iframe that the module cannot see, pause or mute (remote player scripts are not allowed), so a playing YouTube clip keeps its sound when an uploaded video starts and keeps playing when swiped out of the slider.
- **The module** (`assets/anvil-product-videos.js`, 5.5 KB raw, 2.06 KB gzipped, budget 4,096) loads only when at least one card has an uploaded video, whatever the autoplay setting.
- **Pause out of view:** an uploaded video that is playing pauses once it is fully out of view, which includes being swiped out of the slider. This runs in every mode (`mobile`, `all`, `off`), on every width and under save-data, so a video the shopper started with sound never keeps playing unseen.
- **Muted play in view:** `<anvil-product-videos play-muted="mobile|all">`: with `mobile`, below 750 px; with `all`, everywhere. The observer reads `intersectionRatio` with thresholds `[0, 0.25]`: a paused uploaded video at least 25 % in view is set `muted` + `loop` and played, and a muted video stops again below 25 %. The peeking card of a 2-per-row slider (about 20 % in view) therefore stays still. With `mobile`, muted videos stop when the viewport grows past 750 px. It never starts when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set, when `html[data-animations="off"]`, or when the browser asks to save data (`navigator.connection.saveData`). Once the shopper pauses a video, turns its sound on or plays it with sound, it is never started (or muted) again, so rotating a phone across 750 px never cuts the shopper's audio. Native controls stay visible, so every video can be paused (WCAG 2.2.2). The section does **not** use `anvil-media`'s `autoplay` (core's `data-anvil-autoplay`), because the core would restart a video the shopper paused and has no phones-only mode. So only this element starts videos here.
- **One sound at a time:** whatever the autoplay setting, an uploaded video that plays with sound, whether the shopper starts it or unmutes it while it plays (the usual path on phones, where it autoplays muted), pauses every other uploaded video in the section that has sound. Muted clips keep playing. YouTube and Vimeo iframes are not included (see above).
- **Add to cart:** cards whose product can be bought hold a native `{% form 'product' %}` (`id="av-pv-<block id>"`), wrapped by `<anvil-product-form form-id="…">` from `assets/anvil-product.js`. The script is loaded only when such a card exists. Without JavaScript the form posts to `/cart/add` as usual. With it, the shared product form element runs the browser's own validation, then calls `cartAdd(items, {sections: ['anvil-cart-drawer','anvil-cart-bubble'], source: 'add'})`. The drawer opens when the theme says so. The confirmation goes to the open drawer's live region, or to the card's status region, and the button reads "Added to cart" for 2 s. Shopify's 4xx message shows in the card's `role="alert"` box, and any other failure falls back to the native post. Cart-page stores go to the cart after the gift engine settles. Nothing in this section re-implements that logic.
  - single-variant product → hidden `id` input + submit button;
  - product with options and `variant_mode: select` → `<select name="id">` (visually hidden label "Choose a variant: <title>", theme dropdown style). Each option shows the variant title and its price (money filters, following `show_currency_code`), sold-out variants are `disabled` and say so, and the opening choice is `selected_or_first_available_variant`, or `first_available_variant` when that one is sold out (on the product's own page `?variant=` can select a sold-out variant, and a disabled selected option would post no `id`). The single-variant hidden input uses the same variant;
  - `variant_mode: link`, or a product that `requires_selling_plan` → `anvil-button` link "Choose options" to the product;
  - sold-out product → disabled secondary button "Sold out".
  Every button and link carries the product title in visually hidden text ("Add to cart: <title>"), so a list of identical buttons stays distinguishable.
- **Example cards:** a card with no product **and** no video, link or cover (an untouched preset card) shows an example row: placeholder thumbnail, "Example product" (`anvil.card.example_product`), an example price of 19.00 through `anvil-money` (the same example price `anvil-product-card-mini` uses) and a disabled add button. A card with real media and no product shows the video only, because a merchant may want a plain video card. This rule is the same in the editor and on the storefront.
- **Loading:** in the first two sections (or when `section.index` is nil) the first `columns_desktop` cards load eagerly; every other card is lazy.
  - Covers and facade posters: `image_tag` with `loading` from that rule.
  - Uploaded videos in eager cards: `anvil-media` renders `video_tag` directly, so each fetches its single-size poster and its metadata (`preload="metadata"`) when the page is parsed. A video poster cannot be lazy-loaded natively.
  - Uploaded videos in lazy cards are **deferred**: the card holds `[data-pv-defer]` with a lazy `image_tag` poster (`video.preview_image`, the card's `widths` and `sizes`) and the `anvil-media` video markup inside `<noscript>`. When the card comes within 200 px of the view (`IntersectionObserver` `rootMargin: '200px'`), the module parses the noscript text into a `<template>`, points the video's `poster` at the `currentSrc` the poster image already chose (and drops video_tag's fallback `<img>`), and swaps it in. A 12-video section below the fold therefore costs only lazy, correctly sized posters until the shopper gets near it. Without JavaScript the noscript copy renders and sits over the poster in the same grid cell, so the video still plays. In a slider, cards hidden in the track's overflow are clipped from the view, so they mount when they are swiped in; the poster shows until then.
  - Product thumbnails are always lazy (`48, 96, 144`, `sizes: 48px`).
  - `sizes` comes from the column counts and `settings.page_width` / `narrow_width`. The cap clause starts at the viewport width where the cap bites, divided by the column count that applies there: with a page width (1000–1800) it is `(min-width: <cap>px) <cap / desktop columns>px`; with a narrow width below 990 px the desktop clause starts at 990 px and the tablet or phone count divides the cap between `<cap>` px and 990 px (for example narrow 800, 4 / 3 columns: `(min-width: 990px) 200px, (min-width: 800px) 266px, (min-width: 750px) calc(100vw / 3), calc(100vw / 2)`). Only `px` and `calc()` are used, no `min()`.
- **Empty section:** with zero blocks the shopper sees nothing, and the editor shows `anvil.editor.empty_section`.
- **Editor:** `shopify:section:load` re-creates the element, which re-observes its videos and deferred cards. `shopify:block:select` scrolls the slider to the card (component behaviour).
- **CSS:** the `{% stylesheet %}` body is about 2.9 KB raw, 1.0 KB gzipped (limit 6,144 raw). Every selector starts with `.av-product-videos`. It loads `anvil-slider.css` in slider mode. Price, media, field, button and form-message styles come from `anvil-base.css`.

## Honesty notes
- No autoplay with sound: muted only, controls visible, never under reduced motion or save-data, and never again after the shopper pauses, unmutes or plays with sound. YouTube/Vimeo never autoplay. The `autoplay` info says all this in the editor, and that YouTube and Vimeo are not paused by the section.
- "One sound at a time" and "pauses out of view" are claimed for uploaded videos only, because the module cannot control YouTube or Vimeo iframes.
- The `add_label` info: "Name the action only; prices, savings and \"free\" come from Shopify, not from this label."
- Prices, compare-at prices and availability come from Shopify. There is no typed price, saving, stock line or badge field. Sold-out variants cannot be chosen and sold-out products say "Sold out".
- The `video` info asks merchants to show the linked product as it really is, and to say in the title when a creator was paid or got the product free (endorsement disclosure). The `title` info asks for a truthful description.
- Defaults read as examples ("Example heading — …", "Example video — …", "Example product"). The example add button is disabled, so an unedited preset can never add anything to a cart.

## Contract assumptions
- `anvil-media` renders uploaded videos with controls by default and accepts `class`, `alt`, `placeholder`, `widths`, `sizes` and `loading` as documented. The 9:16 box is supplied by this section's CSS (see Open questions).
- `<anvil-product-form>` (assets/anvil-product.js) finds its form through `form-id` when it wraps the form, reads `[data-anvil-form-error]` / `[data-anvil-form-status]` / `[data-anvil-bind="add_label"]` inside itself, and defaults the quantity to 1. The picker's `[data-anvil-bind]` updates are scoped to its own `.shopify-section`, so this section's add label is never touched by a product page's picker.
- `anvil-slider`'s internal `--n` stays the per-view variable (testimonials relies on it too), and the slides read `--av-slider-peek` by inheritance, so the track can override it.
- `anvil-media` renders the noscript copy without scripts (true for its `video` branch), and `video_tag`'s output parses back from the noscript text unchanged.

## Open questions
- **Arrow keys on a focused video (`assets/anvil-slider.js`, not mine).** This is the first section with native `<video controls>` inside `<anvil-slider>`. The slider's keydown handler skips only `input, textarea, select, [contenteditable]`, so ArrowLeft/ArrowRight on a focused video move the slider (and `preventDefault` cancels the seek), after which the video pauses as it scrolls out of view. Needed change by the slider's owner: `e.target.closest('input, textarea, select, video, audio, [contenteditable]')`.
- **`anvil-media` and video names (`snippets/anvil-media.liquid`, not mine).** The facade's play link always reads "Play video" (`anvil.media.play`) and the uploaded-video branch ignores `alt`. Requested: append `alt_text` to the play link's hidden text ("Play video: <title>") and pass `alt` to `video_tag` as the `<video>`'s `aria-label` when given. Until then this section names each card through its always-present `<figcaption>`.
- **`anvil-media` preload and lazy poster (`snippets/anvil-media.liquid`, not mine).** A `preload` param (for `preload: 'none'`) and a lazy-poster option would let this section drop its own deferral (`[data-pv-defer]` + noscript) and serve the `anvil-video` and other video sections the same way.
- **9:16 in `anvil-media`.** The snippet's ratio list stops at `21:9` / `16:9` / `4:5` / `3:4` / `1:1`, so this section overrides `aspect-ratio` on its own media boxes. The `anvil-video` spec card also asks for 9:16. A `9:16` option in the snippet (and `.av-media--9-16` in base CSS) would let both sections drop their overrides.
- **"Muted autoplay on M".** Read as "on phones", so the default is `mobile`, with `all` and `off` as alternatives. If the owner meant the merchant's choice of autoplay in general, the default can become `all` without code changes.
- **`anvil-product.js` on pages without a product section** (about 7.5 KB gzipped, loaded as a deferred module only when a card can add to cart). It is already on product and landing pages, the section's main home. A home-page-heavy store pays for it once. A smaller shared "add one item" element would serve this section, the shoppable image and the Add to cart theme block alike.
- **`blocks/anvil-product-atc.liquid` (not mine, read-only):** it wraps its form in `<anvil-product-form>` without a `form-id` attribute and does not load `anvil-product.js`, so its form is never enhanced and always posts natively. Worth a fix by its owner.
- **Gate script:** `npm run gate -- product-videos` was not run: its paths gate (`scripts/paths-lint.sh`) runs `git status`, which builders in this wave may not run, and the shared tree holds about 45 builders' files at once. The push and screenshot stage needs dev-theme credentials. G1 (theme check) and G2 (cleanroom-lint, asset-audit, chassis-lint, check-limits) were run one by one. Rendering on the dev theme (375 × 812 and 1280 × 800, reduced motion, axe) is still to do.
